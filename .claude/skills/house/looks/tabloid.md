## Parts

- **Newsprint**: `#E4E1D9`, grey-warm (it is NOT cream paper; it is cheap, grey and slightly see-through). The page fills the frame; the desk (`#5B4A3A` wood, 4% grain) shows only at the edges in wide shots.
- **Black ink** `#161616` for everything, set dense.
- **Masthead red** `#D81E05`: the masthead block, and one red "flash" per page (a starburst, an EXCLUSIVE banner, an underline).
- **Photos**: halftone at 85 lpi equivalent (dots 5–7 px), slightly muddy: contrast 115%, blacks lifted 6%. Real photos only.

## Numbers

- Headline: Antonio 700, all caps, tight leading 0.88, tracking −1%, sized so the headline is 2–3 lines and fills 85–95% of page width (typically 190–300 px at 1080p). It is set **justified to the column**, not centred.
- Deck/subhead: Old Standard TT 700 italic, 46 px. Body: Old Standard TT 400, 24 px, in 5 narrow columns with 1 px rules, readable only as texture except the one pulled quote.
- Masthead: reversed white on red, Antonio 700.

## Surface

- Paper show-through: a mirrored, 4%-opacity ghost of the reverse page's layout.
- Ink spread: text-shadow 0 0 0.6px of ink at 60% on headlines.
- One fold across the page at 50% height: a 3 px light-to-dark ridge.

## Motion

- The page is **physical**. Camera moves over it: slow push (0.6% scale per second, linear), and fast reframes between stories (0.35 s `power3.inOut`) like a hand-held glance.
- New headlines don't animate letter by letter; the **page is replaced** (see transitions). Within a page, only the camera and the red flash move: the starburst stamps in at 140% → 100% in 3 frames.
- Pull quotes: a red pen underline drawn in 0.3 s.

## Transitions

- **Page turn**: the page curls from the bottom-right corner (a 0.5 s peel with a moving shadow) to reveal the next page.
- **Slap down**: the next paper is dropped onto the desk on top of the current one (0.25 s fall, 1-frame squash, 3% bounce), slightly rotated.
- **Fold to reveal**: the page folds in half along the fold line, showing the back page.
- Never: the spinning newspaper. It is the single most generated move for this medium.

## Sound

Paper handling (rustle, slap on the desk), a press-hall rumble under the first shot, a camera shutter for a photo reveal. Music: a newsy staccato string figure, or none.

## Voice

Tabloid voice: punny, puffed, short words. `SHOCK`, `AT LAST`, `IT'S OFFICIAL`, `INSIDE: p.4`. Pair with a straight, factual deck underneath, the tension between the two is the joke.

## How this look fails

- A clean newspaper template with tiny centred type. → Huge, dense, justified headlines.
- Cream paper + serif = the banned AI look. → Grey newsprint, condensed sans headline, serif only in body.
- Spinning newspaper. → Banned.
