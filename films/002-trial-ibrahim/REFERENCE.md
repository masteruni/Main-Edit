# Reference study · 002 trial (refs/ref1.mp4, refs/ref2.mp4)

Scope: these references apply to this film only (general edit).

## Files
- ref1: 1276×718, 23.976 fps, 12.5 s. Music with sung/rapped lyrics; captions = lyrics.
- ref2: 1276×720, 24 fps, 20.8 s. Talking-head voiceover (direct to camera) intercut with B-roll; captions = spoken words.
- Both are 16:9 masters; the grammar transfers to our 9:16 Reel.

## Fonts (measured: side-by-side render vs. full-res crops)
| Role | Face | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sans (most words) | **Montserrat Bold** (some ExtraBold) | single-storey g, straight y, wide M. lowercase for running words ("stacked", "right", "my bag"), CAPS for hero numbers/claims ("MILLIONS", "MONTHLY", "DISTRACTED"). |

Sizes (as % of frame height, measured on 720 px): script word 9–11%; sans hero 6–8%; small connector words ("you", "my", "in", "of the") 3–3.5%. Stacked lines overlap: the script line tucks under the sans line by ~10% of its height.

## Caption animation (measured frame by frame)
- **Sync**: every word appears on its own spoken/sung onset (±1 frame). It's word-by-word captioning of the voice, not phrase blocks.
- **Script words**: wipe in left → right over **3 frames** (~0.125 s), like handwriting being revealed; no scale.
- **Sans words**: fade in over **2 frames** (~0.08 s) with a hint of blur; no movement; sometimes cut on in 1 frame.
- **Accumulation**: words build a block for the phrase (3–5 words): staircase layout, each new word under/right of the last, mixed sizes ("stacked / my / *Bread*", "*Stay* in / my bag", "*I'm Telling* / you / right / *Now*"). **The block never moves** once a word is placed.
- **Exit**: the block clears on the next cut or phrase end (cut-off, no animated exit).
- **Placement**: in the negative space beside the subject's head (left or right third), or split across the frame on wide shots ("*One* of the" left · "biggest" right). Never over the face.

## Text effects (measured)
- Fill: warm white (#F5F1EA-ish). Secondary connector words sometimes at ~80% opacity / light grey.
- **Scene tint**: on B-roll the words take the plate's dominant colour: sky-blue "That I'm" over a blue sky (with a soft outer glow of the same blue, ~8–12 px), navy "how they" over a bright desert (dark text when the plate is bright).
- Shadow: very soft dark shadow (~0 2 12px rgba(0,0,0,.4)), no outline, no box.

## Picture & edit grammar (measured)
- Cuts on words; B-roll shots 0.4–1.2 s; talking head returns between B-roll runs.
- Transitions: **light-leak / prism-streak** flashes (3 frames of coloured streaks over black) between phrases; handheld motion blur; one **B&W → colour** pop on the first beat (ref2 frames 0–5 B&W, colour at frame 6).
- Grade: warm practicals, teal/blue shadows, deep blacks, moderate saturation.
- Story: ref2 = a spoken ambition statement ("I'm telling you right now that I'm gonna be one of the biggest…") illustrated by B-roll; ref1 = lyrics carrying a mindset story.

## Apply to this film
- Copy exactly: Montserrat Bold + Parisienne; word-on-onset sync; script L→R wipe 3 f, sans fade 2 f; staircase blocks that never move, cleared on cut; scene-tinted words; soft shadow; light-leak transition between story sections; B&W → colour pop on the hook's first beat.
- Adapt: 9:16 placement (blocks in the upper or lower third beside the head, inside Reels-safe area).
- The story is carried by **Ibrahim's own voiceover** from the footage (ref2 model), not by written slogans.
