# Quick brief

Copy, fill what you know, paste it as one message with your files or links. A filled brief skips the intake questions and the back-and-forth, which saves time and usage. Blank lines are fine: Claude picks a sensible default and says which.

```
EDIT FOR:     general | client: <name> | new client: <name>
TYPE:         edit my footage | motion graphics / animated video | B-roll + graphics over footage | captions only | other: …
FILES:        <Drive links or file names>
PLATFORM:     Reels/TikTok/Shorts 9:16 | Feed 4:5 | YouTube 16:9 | several: …
LENGTH:       ~__ s (max __ s)
LANGUAGE:     English | Tagalog/Taglish | other: …
AUDIO:        keep speaker | replace with VO | music: yes/no/style | sound effects: yes/no
CAPTIONS:     yes/no, style: …
HOOK / GOAL:  what the viewer should feel or do; the line or moment you think should open it
DIRECTION:    the vibe in your words: pacing, energy, what to cut, what to keep
REFERENCES:   links + what you like about each (pacing? captions? colour?). Used for THIS video only unless you write "save to client".
MUST / NEVER: …
DELIVER:      formats, file names, deadline
```
